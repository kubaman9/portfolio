=== V18.3 MARQUEE COVERAGE + COMPANION PARLAY (Jakub, 2026-11-18; replaces V18.2; extends 4A, 4A-1, PARLAY CORNER and 2F; loosens no price, stake or cap rule) ===
Why: Jakub's interest is the marquee games (every Indiana game is marquee). On 2026-11-17 the run priced NHL and NBA only, left 8 Nations League games and 7 of 11 CBB games unpriced, and the marquee games got one 0.1u ticket between them while both slips went to unrelated NBA games. Marquee games should carry bets at a high rate, usually a single plus a companion prop parlay.

V18.3-1. COVERAGE FIRST. Before any time or call budget (playbook daily_run_budget) is spent elsewhere, in this order: (1) price every marquee fixture (every international match incl. Nations League, every Indiana game, every ranked or national-TV CFB/CBB game, every NFL/NBA/NHL/MLB national-TV or playoff game, every fight-card main event) against Pinnacle, game lines AND the player-prop board (SharpAPI event props, then DK/FD); (2) price the whole CBB board against Pinnacle in one call; (3) pull injuries/goalies/lineups for the marquee games. The budget may trim depth on other games, never these three steps. Early kickoffs (Nations League 2:45 PM ET, European club matches, noon CFB) are priced at the morning run. If props are not posted yet, say so and re-check at 3C pre-kick.

V18.3-2. MARQUEE BET RATE. Target: at least 80% of marquee games on the slate carry a position (single and/or parlay), and every Indiana game carries one whenever any market is posted. For each marquee game take the best-priced side among game lines and props; it is sized by its own quality under 2C (a priced edge gets its normal tier; no edge = 0.1u, pick_grade "forced", labelled honestly). A marquee pass is allowed only with a written reason in the run note ("pass: <game>: <reason>", e.g. no market posted, every price worse than -4% EV, injury news pending), and every pass shows on the site under "Priced, no bet". The 8u daily ceiling, the stake ladder, the anchor rules and the immutable list still bind; a forced marquee ticket never counts as an edge in the record notes.

V18.3-3. COMPANION PARLAY. Every marquee game that carries a single gets ONE companion same-game parlay (slip_type "sgp", tags prop_parlay + companion), built on the same game script as the single:
   - Prefer 2-3 player-prop legs (different players, different markets) that agree with the single's read. Example: IU spread single -> IU primary scorer over points + opposing guard under assists.
   - No props posted: build it from game markets on that game (alt spread/ML + total, team totals, first half) that express the same script.
   - Size follows the single: single 0.5u or more -> companion 0.25u if it clears its break-even, else 0.1u STRETCH; single 0.25u -> companion 0.1u (0.25u only if every leg would be a STANDARD-or-better single on its own); single 0.1u forced -> companion 0.1u STRETCH when a coherent script exists, else skip with a reason. Indiana games always get the companion when any market is posted.
   - Companions come before generic slips: on a day with N marquee singles the slip count may rise to N companions + 1 other slip, max 5 slips (PARLAY CORNER max), still inside the 8u ceiling. All PARLAY CORNER constraints bind (no shared legs across slips, no same-market legs on one game, naive price + break-even + multiplied leg probabilities, STRETCH label below break-even).
   - Skip only when the game has no posted market or every candidate leg fails the hard constraints; write "no companion: <game>: <reason>".

V18.3-4. BOARD DATA (the site's Today page is a board grouped by game: what was on, what was priced, what was bet). In the 6A dashboard_days write:
   - Every slip carries matchup ("Away Team @ Home Team", same spelling as the single on that game), game_time, book, marquee (true/false), companion_of (the single's bet text), wrong_if, line_gap_thesis, and legs_detail[] with selection, market, price_american, fair_prob (devigged percent) and status.
   - Every single carries marquee true/false (true for every Indiana game).
   - full_slate gets ONE ROW PER GAME PRICED: {game: "Away @ Home", sport, time, tier "MARQUEE"/"WORTH WATCHING"/"THE REST", line: key DK vs Pinnacle numbers, read: best price found and its EV, then "bet" or "pass: <reason>"}. At most one summary row for games not priced; unpriced leagues go in leagues_unchecked.

V18.3-5. REPORT LINES in 4A and the run note: "marquee: {bet}/{total} games bet ({pct}%), passes: <list with reason>" and "companions: {N} marquee singles -> {M} companion slips (skipped: <list>)". The weekly review reports marquee and companion tickets separately (tags marquee, companion) so the data says whether they earn; if marquee forced tickets lose more than 3u over 4 weeks the review may propose lowering the 80% target (instruction_proposals only).
